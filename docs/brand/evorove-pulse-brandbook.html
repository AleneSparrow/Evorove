01 — Essence

EVERY LEAD.
NO DEAD AIR.

Evorove is a deterministic engine that carries an inbound inquiry through qualification, follow-up, and a booked job or accepted quote. The model rewrites a script the business already approved. The engine decides the next step. It is not a chatbot, not an intake form, not lead generation.

The object

A torus. A closed cycle. The lead enters and does not fall off the ring. Inner hub = decision. Tube = the work. Lime orbit = follow-up still firing.

The room

Warm cream, not a dark “AI void.” Industrial and expensive, like a studio for a physical object. Negative space is a feature.

The temperature

Coral is heat. Lime is the live wire. Ink is the law. If a layout is missing one of the first two, it is not Pulse yet — it is a draft.

02 — Mark

TORUS, NOT A WHEEL.

The 2D mark is a flat translation of the 3D hero: thick ring, solid hub, a short motion arc at 1–2 o’clock. Never five spokes. Never bronze. Never a chat bubble.

Construction

Viewbox 24. Ring radius 7.2, stroke 3.2. Hub radius 2.2, filled. Optional motion arc: a 9-unit sweep, stroke 1.6, 45% opacity. Sit the mark in a coral disc for badges. Favicon drops the arc — it dies at 16px.

Clear space & crop

Keep a gap of one hub-diameter around the disc. Profile photos are round: never put the wordmark in the avatar. Name lives in the account field. The lockup with EVOROVE under the disc is for square surfaces only (GitHub, press sheets).

05 — Voice

DRY HEAT.
NO THEATER.

English everywhere a customer can see it. Short sentences. Physical metaphors (ring, cycle, dead air). We do not promise a conversion rate. We do not call the first vertical “the product.”

Say

Inquiry in. Booked job out. The model rewrites. The engine decides. Same engine, new DNA. Dead air is a product bug. Unfinished — on purpose. Building in public. On your script.

Never

Chatbot. Intake assistant. Cold lead. CRM replacement. “AI lawyer.” “Compliant with TCPA.” A percentage we will hit for you. Custom build for every vertical. Five-spoke flywheel nostalgia in public copy.

06 — Don’t

RETIRED MOVES.

Five-spoke wheel

The old Flywheel emblem. Bronze #B87333. Century Gothic. All three are archive-only.

Chat as the brand

No bubbles, no robot heads, no handshake stock. The torus is the only object. Conversation is a channel, not the identity.

coral 12px on cream fails

Pretty but illegal contrast

Small coral on white/cream is not readable enough. Use coral deep, or coral only at display size.

EVOROVE

Lime as a field for the name

Lime is a sticker and a spark, not a wall. Profile and app icon stay coral or ink.

08 — Multimedia

THE ENGINE
AS AN OBJECT.

Campaign photography is one thing: a glossy coral torus, lime orbit, cream studio, no people. Type sits beside it or over a cream veil. Feed posts are typographic posters — couplet + sticker + one sentence.

Banners

LinkedIn cream + lime sticker. X ink + cream wordmark. YouTube splits type and the 3D still. Safe zone: keep the name left of center.

Feed

1200×675 posters. One idea each: cycle vs capture, engine vs model, DNA vs custom build, dead air, unfinished. No screenshots of the cabinet in launch posts.

Stories / Reels

1080×1920. Display type in the lower third or over a cream veil. Motion: 9s torus spin, lime sparks, sticker tilt −2°.
